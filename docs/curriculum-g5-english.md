Level: **Basic User I (A1) / Foundation**. Vocabulary: **Band I** (Core I = grade 4 to mid-grade 5; Core II = mid-grade 5 to end of grade 6). The Band I word list is published as an Excel file linked from the curriculum.

## How the curriculum is built
English is skills-based, not topic-based: the curriculum lists what a learner *can do* (listening, reading, speaking, writing) with Band I vocabulary and a growing set of grammar structures. The app covers what can be practised alone on a phone: vocabulary, grammar, reading and spelling. Speaking, listening and free writing stay with the teacher.

## Content notes
- Questions, options and examples are in **English**. Lessons, hints and explanations are in **Hebrew** (with English examples), as in Israeli grade 5 textbooks.
- The pack is marked `dir: 'ltr'`. Question cards, lessons and the parent's mistake list use the `dir-auto` class: each paragraph, option and line takes its direction from its own text.
- New answer type: **typed word** (`text`). The answer is compared without case, niqqud, curly vs. straight apostrophes or end punctuation; each item lists every accepted spelling (for example `can't`, `cannot`).
- Bank items are marked `wordProblem` like all bank items. English prompts write times as `7:30`, never `7.30`, to keep the decimal rule.
